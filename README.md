# Key Insights
-Product Insights
-Accessories achieve a high profit margin of more than 50%, while Components have a much lower margin of approximately 6.9%.
-The product mix differs considerably between regions, with Southwest generating substantially higher Components sales than -Australia.
-Bikes are the largest contributor to profit in both Southwest and Australia.
# Region Insights
-Southwest generates the highest sales at approximately $24M, but Australia generates higher profit at approximately $3.5M compared with Southwest's approximately $2.3M.
-Australia achieves a substantially higher profit margin, at approximately 33%, compared with approximately 9% in Southwest.
-Australia has a lower Cost % of Sales than Southwest across all product categories, indicating better cost efficiency relative to sales and contributing to its stronger profitability.
# Customer Insights
- **Jordan is the highest-revenue customer and is also the highest-profit customer.
- **Most of the customers appearing among the highest-revenue customers also appear among the highest-profit customers, although their rankings differ.
- **A significant portion of sales is associated with unregistered or unidentified customers, so these sales should be analyzed separately from identifiable customer performance.
# Actionable Recommendations
- **	Reprice Negative-Margin Models: Re-evaluate the list price of models generating negative margins to ensure direct unit costs are fully covered
- **	Push High-Margin Inventory: Realign marketing and sales efforts toward high-margin products (e.g., Road-350-W Yellow) to restore previous profitability levels.
